Custom Learning Programs

Bring several capabilities together in a learning program designed around your organisation’s requirements.

Discuss team training

A considered approach to capability.

Different roles often need different levels of knowledge and practice. A custom learning program can connect those requirements through a coherent set of objectives, activities and resources.

The engagement begins by clarifying the audience and the work the learning should support. The proposal can then set out the agreed subject areas, sequence, format, preparation and review arrangements.

Who this is for

Organisations with a defined capability need that extends beyond a single subject or session.

Engagement scope

What the learning can cover.

The final scope is agreed around your audience and requirements.

  • Audience and capability requirements
  • Role-specific learning outcomes
  • The sequence and depth of subject areas
  • Practical activities and supporting resources
  • Delivery, review and evaluation arrangements

Define a useful result.

Agree the intended outputs and review arrangements before the engagement begins.

  • An agreed learning scope and program structure
  • A proposed sequence for the intended audience
  • Defined delivery requirements and review points

Prepare for the discussion.

Share the capability challenge, groups involved, preferred timeframes and any existing learning material or technology constraints.

Include timing, location or format preferences, together with any technical, accessibility or information-handling requirements.
