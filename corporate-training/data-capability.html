Data Capability for Teams

Strengthen the way your people question, interpret and communicate business information.

Discuss team training

A considered approach to capability.

Useful analysis begins with a well-defined question and an understanding of the information available. This training area develops the habits that help people interpret business data with greater care.

A scoped program can combine data literacy with relevant spreadsheet or reporting practices. The emphasis is on drawing a sound conclusion, explaining its limitations and presenting it in a form the intended audience can use.

Who this is for

Teams that use reports, spreadsheets or operational data to support decisions.

Engagement scope

What the learning can cover.

The final scope is agreed around your audience and requirements.

  • Framing an answerable business question
  • Recognising missing, inconsistent or incomplete data
  • Selecting useful summaries and comparisons
  • Choosing clear charts and report structures
  • Communicating findings without overstating the evidence

Define a useful result.

Agree the intended outputs and review arrangements before the engagement begins.

  • A shared approach to asking questions of data
  • Practical checks for common data-quality issues
  • Clearer conventions for presenting business findings

Prepare for the discussion.

Describe the reports and decisions your team works with. Any examples supplied for learning activities should be appropriately de-identified and approved for use.

Include timing, location or format preferences, together with any technical, accessibility or information-handling requirements.
