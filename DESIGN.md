# Wisme Education visual system

Approved reference: Executive mockup A, selected by the owner. Later direction: B2B priority, small imagery, a more substantial website, and faithful use of the homepage’s visual system on every interior page.

- Navy `#102b42`; heading ink `#0b1930`; teal `#087a7c`; dark teal `#076467`.
- White dominates; pale `#f5f8fb` separates selected sections; border `#dce5ec`.
- Inter is self-hosted. Main headings use 550–650 weight; body uses regular weight and generous line height.
- Maximum content width: 1180px. Mobile margins: 20px. Desktop section spacing: 76px; mobile: 52px.
- Header, footer, buttons, breadcrumb hierarchy, link styling and page headers are shared across every page.
- Homepage retains the selected compact book sculpture, navy service band, individual-learning route and restrained teal closing section.
- Program catalogue uses horizontal subject filters and wide, softly rounded pale program panels. Two columns on desktop become one on mobile.
- Service directories use wide pale panels with compact line icons and clear links. Related programs and article previews use the same panel geometry.
- Program pages have a compact enquiry rail; it moves between the facts and main content on mobile.
- Legal pages use numbered headings, a contents rail and a narrow reading measure. Their wording must remain understandable and accurate.
- Primary buttons are teal with white text. Keyboard focus is visible. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- No invented testimonials, client logos, accreditation, scale claims, offices, team members or prices.
- No watermarks or preview banners. Form labels describe the real review/copy action.


## Interior design alignment

The selected Executive homepage remains the visual source of truth. Primary buttons use its teal fill, 8px corners, medium/semibold labels and restrained arrows. Header Enquire uses the same filled treatment without an arrow. Secondary buttons use a fine teal outline. Navy bands, pale 14px rounded panels and compact image frames are reused purposefully across the inner-page templates.

Programs, Corporate Training and Academy have coordinated page designs. Commercial detail pages add a navy enquiry or scope section. Articles and policies retain readable content columns within the same typography, spacing and navigation system. Images are capped at 320px wide and 208px high in interior desktop headers, and 150px high on small screens.
