Program overview

Develop a practical way to work with data, from asking a focused question to explaining what the results mean. Give equal attention to data quality, analytical thinking and communication.

Who it is for

Professionals building their confidence with business data.

What you’ll develop

  • Frame an answerable business question.
  • Recognise common data quality issues.
  • Select appropriate summaries and visualisations.
  • Explain findings alongside their limitations.

Program outline

Explore the topics covered in this program area.

01Questions and data

Connect a business question with the information needed to answer it.

02Preparing information

Inspect missing values, inconsistent labels and common errors.

03Exploring patterns

Use summaries and comparisons to investigate a question.

04Communicating findings

Choose clear charts and explain what the evidence supports.
