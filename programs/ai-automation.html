Program overview

Understand how automation connects tasks, tools and information. Explore where AI can add value, where predictable rules work better and how to keep people in control of important decisions.

Who it is for

Business professionals and team leads exploring workflow automation.

What you’ll develop

  • Map the steps and hand-offs in a business process.
  • Distinguish rules-based automation from AI-assisted tasks.
  • Identify exceptions, risks and approval points.
  • Outline a small automation pilot with a measurable goal.

Program outline

Explore the topics covered in this program area.

01Mapping a workflow

Document inputs, decisions, outputs and the people involved.

02Choosing an opportunity

Assess repetition, effort, reliability and sensitivity of information.

03Connecting tools

Explore triggers, actions and data hand-offs at a practical level.

04Testing and oversight

Plan a small pilot with exception handling and human review.
