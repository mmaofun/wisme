Program overview

Build a clear foundation in artificial intelligence without getting lost in technical language. Explore common applications, recognise limitations and develop a considered approach to using AI in a professional setting.

Who it is for

Professionals exploring AI and its relevance to their work.

What you’ll develop

  • Explain the difference between AI, machine learning and generative AI.
  • Identify tasks where AI could support your work.
  • Recognise accuracy, privacy and bias considerations.
  • Ask better questions when evaluating an AI tool.

Program outline

Explore the topics covered in this program area.

01Understanding AI

Explore the concepts behind AI and the kinds of tasks different tools can support.

02AI in the workplace

Consider applications in communication, research, analysis and routine administration.

03Limits and responsible use

Examine unreliable outputs, sensitive information and the importance of human review.

04Choosing an opportunity

Use a simple framework to assess a practical use case for your role.
