Our learning approach

Learning connected to professional practice.

Clear concepts, relevant activities and a considered approach to applying knowledge at work.

Build capability with purpose.

Learning is easier to place in context when its purpose is clear. The audience should understand what a topic is intended to help them do and how it connects with their role.

Wisme’s approach centres on understanding, practical application and reflection. The emphasis and activities are shaped around the program’s objectives and the audience’s starting point.

01

Understand

Build a clear foundation in the concepts, tools and decisions relevant to your work.

02

Apply

Connect what you learn with realistic tasks and professional situations.

03

Improve

Reflect on the results and refine an approach you can use again.

Relevant examples and activities.

Examples should help participants connect a concept with familiar professional situations. Activities provide a way to examine an approach, make a decision or practise a defined task.

Appropriate human review is particularly important when working with AI or business information.

A clear learning expectation.

Program outcomes help define the intended areas of development. Confirm suitability, prerequisites, materials and any assessment or completion arrangements before participation.
