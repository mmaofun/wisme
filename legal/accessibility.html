Accessibility

Wisme Education aims to make its website clear, readable and usable for a wide range of visitors.

On this page

1. Our approach2. Keyboard navigation3. Reading and display preferences4. Forms and interactions5. Reporting a barrier6. Continued improvement

1. Our approach

The site uses a consistent structure, readable text, labelled controls and visible keyboard focus. Content is designed to adapt to different screen sizes and text settings. Accessibility is an ongoing part of maintaining the website.

2. Keyboard navigation

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links and controls. A skip link provides direct access to the main content. The mobile menu can be closed with Escape. Expandable questions and program outlines use native disclosure controls.

3. Reading and display preferences

Text can be enlarged through your browser. The site respects a reduced-motion preference and keeps essential content available without animation. Most written content can be read with JavaScript disabled.

4. Forms and interactions

Fields have visible labels and validation messages. Enquiry information can be reviewed before copying it or opening an email draft when a contact address is available. Some enhanced interactions require JavaScript.

5. Reporting a barrier

If a page or control is difficult to use, describe the page, the problem and the browser or assistive technology involved through the contact channel available to you. Share only details needed to explain the barrier.

6. Continued improvement

This statement describes the design approach and is not a claim of independent accessibility certification. Feedback and testing should inform future improvements.
