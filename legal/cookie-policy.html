Cookies & Website Technology

A straightforward explanation of the technologies used by this website.

On this page

1. What cookies do2. Current website features3. Analytics and advertising4. Hosting and external services5. Managing your browser6. Future changes7. Related information

1. What cookies do

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser at a website’s request. They can support functions such as sign-in, preferences or analytics. Different websites use them for different purposes.

2. Current website features

The current Wisme website does not set application cookies or use browser storage for enquiry fields. Program filtering uses a page URL parameter. Menu and form states are held within the current page.

3. Analytics and advertising

No analytics, advertising pixels or marketing tracking scripts are included in the current site. There is no marketing subscription associated with the enquiry form.

4. Hosting and external services

Hosting providers may process technical request information as part of operating the service. If you follow a link to another website or open an email application, the provider’s own technology and privacy practices apply.

5. Managing your browser

Your browser’s settings let you inspect, remove or restrict stored site information. These controls may affect other websites differently. Check your browser’s help for instructions.

6. Future changes

Any future analytics or marketing technology should be reviewed before it is introduced. This page and any appropriate consent controls must be updated to reflect the actual tools in use.

7. Related information

Read the Privacy Policy for information about enquiry preparation and hosting.
